Claude Desktop

Claude Desktop is a personal, single-user tool. It lets the developer's own Claude assistant read and update the developer's own Google Drive files, Sheets, Docs, Calendar and Gmail through Google's official APIs, from the developer's own computer.

It is not a commercial product, it is not offered to the public, and it has no other users. It does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
