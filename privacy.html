Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy covers Claude Desktop, a personal tool used only by its developer to connect their own Claude assistant to their own Google account.

What Google data the app accesses

With the account owner's consent on Google's sign-in screen, the app can access the Google services the owner approves: Drive files, Sheets, Docs, Calendar, Contacts, Tasks and Gmail in the owner's own account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out tasks the account owner asks their assistant to do, such as reading a spreadsheet or updating a file. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training models, and is not sold.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The app keeps no separate database or server copy of Google data.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with or transferred to anyone, except as needed to carry out the owner's own requests through their assistant.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/dividedby/claude-desktop-oauth.
